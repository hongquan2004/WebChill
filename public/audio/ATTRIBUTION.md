# Nature recordings / Nguồn bản thu thiên nhiên

These four field recordings are bundled locally as Freesound public HQ MP3 previews. Each source page identifies its recording as Creative Commons Zero (CC0 1.0), checked on 2026-10-05.

- **stream.mp3** — [Small Stream Ambience 1](https://freesound.org/people/deadrobotmusic/sounds/687058/) by **deadrobotmusic**, 46.567 seconds. [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).
- **wind.mp3** — [Wind in trees.WAV](https://freesound.org/people/nickydunne/sounds/463551/) by **nickydunne**, 90.814 seconds. [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).
- **rain.mp3** — [Soft Rain Ambience edlarez vsnr.wav](https://freesound.org/people/visionear/sounds/573335/) by **visionear**, 92.376 seconds. [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).
- **birds.mp3** — [Forest birds - ambient seamless loop](https://freesound.org/people/Magnesus/sounds/723913/) by **Magnesus**, 27.051 seconds. [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).

The downloaded MP3 files are unchanged. During playback the app builds an equal-power crossfade at loop boundaries and adjusts gain conservatively. The wind recording includes a nearby small river and distant birds, as described by its creator; these sounds cannot be isolated by the bird slider. Source URLs, download URLs, sizes and SHA-256 checksums are in manifest.json.

If a file cannot load or decode, that layer uses locally synthesized fallback sound. The completion chime is synthesized separately. No recordings load before sound is enabled, and runtime playback contacts only this app's origin.
